# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are prospective clients and collaborators evaluating Erinç Polat as an experienced freelance software developer. They may arrive in Turkish or English and often need to assess credibility, technical range, relevant work, and fit for a new project quickly.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Erinç Polat's software-development capabilities, selected project work, delivery approach, and contact options. Success means a visitor can understand the offer, find relevant evidence, and confidently start a conversation.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines web, cross-platform mobile, backend, infrastructure, IoT, and product-design experience with concrete project examples. It should demonstrate breadth through shipped work and execution details rather than generic technology claims or hype.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a bilingual Turkish/English portfolio across desktop and mobile, typically moving from the home-page overview into project summaries or detailed case studies before contacting Erinç. The primary journey is credibility assessment followed by project inquiry; interactive and 3D demonstrations are supporting evidence, not the main task.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Turkish and English routes must remain equivalent and visually consistent.
- The site includes a portfolio home page, project listing and detail pages, a contact form, downloadable résumé, and selected interactive/3D demonstrations.
- The implementation uses Next.js, React, MUI, next-intl, Framer Motion, and React Three Fiber where needed.
- Accessibility must include semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, meaningful labels and alternative text, sufficient contrast, zoom support, and readable responsive layouts.
- Existing factual copy, project claims, client relationships, and contact details must not be invented or expanded without confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Erinç Polat.
- Primary logo asset: `public/logo.webp`.
- Voice: clear, capable, approachable, reliable, innovative, and grounded in real delivery experience.
- The experience should build trust while communicating technical sophistication and active interest in new product ideas.
- The current product is light-first; dark mode is not an established requirement.
- Buttons and CTAs must be shadow-free in every interaction state. The user prefers emphasis through color and contrast, not button shadows or elevation; preserve visible keyboard-focus outlines.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project and case-study content in the localized message files and project routes.
- Project imagery under `public/projects/` and `public/welcomeSectionAssets/`.
- Portrait asset at `public/erinc-polat.webp`.
- Downloadable résumé at `public/erinc-polat-resume.pdf`.
- Service imagery under `public/services/` and the footer artwork at `public/footer.webp`.
- No testimonials, performance benchmarks, awards, or additional client claims should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Earn trust quickly by making experience, services, project outcomes, and contact paths easy to understand and verify.
2. Let real projects and outcomes lead; interface elements should frame the work rather than compete with it.
3. Show innovation through purposeful execution, interaction, and technical demonstrations rather than novelty.
4. Keep the bilingual experience consistent and the contact journey direct.
5. Design inclusively across screen sizes, languages, keyboard input, assistive technology, zoom, and reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA contrast as the minimum for text and interactive controls. Preserve browser zoom, provide keyboard focus and accessible names, associate form errors with their fields, and avoid relying on color alone to communicate meaning.
